And the row kept for the record: the same six towers laid **chassis first**
win Standard **46%** where depth first wins 93%. The order was the instrument's
mistake for six sessions; it is what a new player will do, and half of them
will lose for it. That is a fact about the *prices* worth his eye, not a
defect in a number: D37 chose an expensive chassis on purpose.

## "The tree wins Grim" is two different sentences

Every record of 2026-09-18 says the tree wins Grim 99–100%, and the gate
holds it there (`S4-grim-tree`, 90–100%). That reading is **six hand-picked
relics — `hot_loads`, `quick_hands`, `iron_sights`, `deep_cold`,
`thick_walls`, `second_wind` — all held at EPIC from wave 1**. The band's own reason calls it a
ceiling, and it is one.

The ladder deals its tree rungs the other way: eight seeded sets of six
relics drawn from the pool the epic band allows, each held at a rarity rolled
by the offer's own mix (common 50, rare 30, epic 15 of 95). On those:

| Grim, the tree, six relics **dealt** | wins |
|---|---|
| a Laser in the Mortar's slot | **80%** |
| the mixed tree line (Tesla, Frost, Railbore, Laser, Mortar) | 49% |
| the base line (Frost, Railbore, Mortar, Railbore) | **46%** |
| a Tesla in the slot | 40% |
| a Missile Rack in the slot | 38% |
| mono-energy | 20% |
| *for scale — the base line holding NOTHING under the tree* | *23%* |

So a player who has bought the whole workshop and plays the base line wins
Grim about **half** the time on relics like the ones a run deals, not 99%;
and the dealt six are still held from wave 1, which a real run never does, so
even 46% flatters it. **Between 46% and 99% lies the question of what Grim is
supposed to be for a player with the whole tree**, and nobody has stated that
target: S4 says "≥ 60%", which the Laser's line meets and the base line does
not. *State it before tuning anything against it.*

**And the Laser.** With relics dealt, a Laser in the slot is worth **+34
points** over the Mortar it replaces (80% against 46%), where the Tesla and
the Missile Rack are worth −6 and −8. Holding nothing the gap is +18 (41%
against 23%). A2 as written — *no slot over 70% holding nothing* — is met;
A2 as meant — *none of them is the answer to everything* — is not obviously
met: on Grim the tree currently has one answer. Whether that is the Laser
being too good or its siblings too plain is the arsenal's open half (#366,
#392), and this table is the rung it will be read on.

## What this read does not say

- **The human offset.** Every rate is the lab's player, who buys the instant
  it can afford and places by arithmetic. Nobody has played any of it.
- **Relics arrive over a run.** "Held" and "dealt" both mean *from wave 1*.
- **The 200-seed slot readings of the arsenal record** predate #367's fix;
  this read (120 seeds, after it) has Mortar 23 / Tesla 20 / Missile 16 /
  Laser 41 where that one had 21 / 19 / 14 / 42 — the same picture.
