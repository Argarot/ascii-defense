# Build pads - the census of the rule (Rework I, PR 1)

*2026-09-19. PRD sec 32.1, D42. The instrument: `node tools/pad-census.mjs 500`
(`packages/harness/src/lab/padCensus.ts`) - 500 of the app's own maps a Threat,
7x5 tiles, the pad rule spending the map stream's last dice exactly as the app
spends them. **A prototype behind a switch: nothing here is tuned, and nothing
is until Daniil has played three runs and said the density feels right.***

## The targets, written before the rule (PRD sec 32.1)

1. a 7x5-tile board opens with **25-35 pads**;
2. **3-5 of them on the last shared stretch** by the Core;
3. **no lane without a pad in reach.**

**How to read it honestly.** Targets 1 and 2 are met *by construction*: the
count is the knob times the board's tiles, and the stretch draws 3 to 5. What
the census could have failed and did not: a board with too little ground in
reach of road to fill the count (never, in 1,500 - a board has about 600 ground
cells and keeps one in twenty); a Core approach with fewer than three ground
cells beside it (never); and target 3, a lane whose own road has no ground
within two cells (never, of 12,608 lanes).

**What the rule makes of a board.** About six hundred ground cells become
25-35 pads; everything else that was ground is bedrock. A cell beside a
*straight* road touches three road cells, so "touches 3-4" is the ordinary
roadside pad - half of them. **About a quarter sit where a pad sees the most
road - the inside of a bend, a crossing, the cell between two lanes that touch
(5 road cells and up): seven a board on Standard, nine on Calm, five on Grim.**
Those are the positions sec 32.1 wants worth owning, and the special tiles
(sec 4.8) are where the best of them are. One or two a board are set back from
the road - a Mortar's, whose dead zone wants the distance; boon ground is
always a pad, wherever it was dealt.

**The bias knob has room to work**: at bias 3 the share at bends goes 24% -> 38%
(100 seeds). It was not moved: which mix *plays* right is the gate's question.

## Looked at, in the running game (the dev build, a 7x4 board in the pane)

- At the Threats' own density, at 0.46 a tile (13 pads) and at 1.36 (38): the
  per-tile knob scales to the board the screen gives (28 tiles, not 35).
- **The first look changed the drawing.** Plain ground beside a road read as a
  bulge of the road, and at thirteen pads they could not be found at a glance.
  Under the switch an empty pad now wears a quiet tint of the hover's own "you
  may build here" green (`terrain.pad.dark`). Bedrock has its own darker roles
  and no mass-edge shading, so the pads are what the eye finds.
- A tower builds on a pad and is refused on bedrock (`canBuild` false / true;
  `build` false / true). The inspector says "bedrock - nothing is built here,
  and nothing is under it".

## What this does not know

- **Whether 25-35 feels like a puzzle or a cage.** His three runs.
- **Rock is untouched** - it still hides ore, a cache or nothing, and is still
  prospected. Digging (sec 32.2) is the next piece and re-reads every rock.
- **The lab has not played it, on purpose**: every plan places towers on
  ground that no longer exists. The re-fit is Rework IV.
- The Core strip's ground is bedrock too, bar the pads the stretch keeps; the
  cells that touch the face weigh three times in that draw, so the gift cells
  (sec 4.5) are usually pads and not always.
