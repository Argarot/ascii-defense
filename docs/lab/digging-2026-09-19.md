# Digging - the first reading (Rework I, PR 2)

*2026-09-19. PRD sec 32.2, D43. Instruments: `packages/harness/src/lab/dig.test.ts`
(the rules, cell by cell) and `node tools/rework-probe.mjs 40` (a named plan on
the app's own maps with the prototype's switch off and on). **A prototype
behind a switch: a reading, never a tuning.***

## What is built, under the switch

One rock; **a pad under every one**; boon ground under the rocks the map dealt a
find, and nothing else - no vein, no cache. **One layer of sight**: rock and
bedrock are one mass until an open cell (road, pad, vein, the Core) touches
them; a finished dig opens the cells around it - the tunnel. **Flat 60 Scrap,
45 seconds**, paid when ordered. **One crew**, the rest of the queue waits (four
deep). Second Crew is the Refinery's *Automation* fork; *Survey* reveals the
ground within two cells of its Refinery; the Quarry relic is the Seismograph
(two layers of sight); Prospector's Eye halves a dig; Vein Tap builds only on
rock the player can see. Their cards say so while the switch is on.

**Not built:** the Seismograph's legendary tier (the whole board); the *Work
Gang* relic (sec 32.2 leaves "one source of crews or both" to the lab); corner
marks on a rock with a boon under it (the inspector says it in words). The
codex still shows the shipped sentences for the four re-read cards.

## The reading - Standard, the mixed line bought depth first, 40 seeds

| row | wins | mean death wave of the losses | towers standing at the end | digs ordered |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the game as it is | 90% | 15.5 | 24.6 | 0 |
| pads on, never digs | 53% | 16.9 | 21.8 | 0 |
| pads on, digs at every chance | 8% | 10.4 | 5.6 | 7.8 |

1. **The map bounds the build now.** Thirty pads and nothing else changed: the
   line that wins Standard 90% wins it 53%. That is the rework's premise
   measured, and it is why every band is invalid the day the switch becomes the
   game (Rework IV re-fits; nothing is fitted here).
2. **At the first-pass density the line never runs out of pads**: it ends on
   about 22 towers with about 30 pads to stand on. So a dig is not bought for
   *count* - it is bought for *position*: the cell that touches both lanes.
3. **Digging greedily loses the run.** The third row's player orders a dig
   whenever 60 Scrap is in the purse, and dies at wave 10 holding five towers.
   A dig competes with a tower for the same purse, which is what makes it a
   decision - and it means sec 32.2's target, *"a run that digs at every chance
   ends about six pads up"*, **cannot be read off this player**: the lab's
   placement is greedy over the cells that exist, and cannot value a cell that
   does not exist yet. The honest bound: one crew finishes at most 17 digs in a
   Standard run (800 s / 45 s), six digs are 360 Scrap, and the greedy player
   had ordered 7.8 by the wave it died on. Whether three to six digs a run
   *feels* right is the gate's question - his hands.

- Anything about Calm or Grim; anything about the tree's towers on pads.
