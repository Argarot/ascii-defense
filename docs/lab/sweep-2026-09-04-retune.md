## Readings against the morning table

- **Mortar: the fork is honest now.** On 7×5 the Wide Burst line ties
  (Concussive 7.0, Cluster 7.0); on 12×7 Cluster edges Concussive on the
  Wide Burst side (8.3–8.5 vs 8.3). The Shaped Charge side still leans
  Concussive (9 vs 7 on one seed) - a smaller blast squared with a scatter
  is the mechanism, and that is a real trade, not a dominated one.
- **Bolt: Hailstorm gained a wave and still loses to Railbore on every
  seed** (7×5 means 10.5 vs 14.0 on the Marksman/Piercing line). 60% × 3
  shots is 180% of one shot on paper; solo against a stream the third shot
  often has no third target, and Railbore's armour bypass is worth more
  than its +22 the later the wave. The next step is either 75% per shot or
  a mixed-build sweep where a Hailstorm line sits behind a Frost - the
  crowd it is written for. Daniil's call.
- Frost unchanged (not retuned): Shatterfield still dominates solo; the
  mixed-build sweep is still what would judge Absolute Zero.
