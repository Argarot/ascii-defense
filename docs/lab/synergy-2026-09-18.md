## Two relics, and why each is the shape it is

- **Payload** — *"A tower whose hit is under 10 hits for 11 more - before any
  multiplier. Nothing for a big gun."* A new fold knob, `damageAdd`, with its
  limit `damageAddBelow`. Three decisions, each forced by a reading:
  - **Flat, before the multipliers**, so Hot Loads multiplies the sum:
    (8 + 11) × 1.15 where it was 8 × 1.15. That product is the "find".
  - **Not `mods: { damage }`**, which the ticket said would do with no engine
    change. It would have made a plain Frost — 0 damage, pure control — a
    damage tower. The knob skips a hit of zero.
  - **Only a tower whose OWN hit is under 10.** Un-gated, the flat bonus
    lifted the base world's mixed line on Grim as gladly as it lifted width:
    32% at +3, 71% at +8. With the limit a Railbore (30), a finished Frost
    (18) and a Shaped Charge (22) get nothing. "Own" is the tower's def and
    tiers, before any gift, aura or relic — the first build read the folded
    hit, and a Bolt beside a Bastion (8 × 1.15 = 9.2, × 1.45 = 11.6) would
    have lost its relic by standing next to its own support. **Under 10 and
    not "10 or less"**: a plain Mortar's blast is exactly 10.
- **Penetrators** — *"Every hit ignores 2 of what a body wears - armour,
  insulation and plating alike."* `armorPierce`; copies add. Type-blind, like
  plating. It is held low on purpose: pierce is the half of the pair a Railbore
  also enjoys, and at 4 it took the Grim guard to 37%.

Both common, both stackable, tags `damage`. Rare / epic: Payload 13 / 16,
Penetrators 3 / 4 — **not fitted**: the base world deals commons alone (D29),
and the tree's bands are for #366 to read.

## How it was found

**1. A bounding round before any tuning** — can the mechanism meet the target
at *any* value? (un-gated Payload; 80 seeds)

| Payload / Penetrators | +3 / 2 | +8 / 5 | +16 / 8 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard, plain Bolts holding the set | 1% | 99% | 100% |
| …Payload alone / Penetrators alone / the pair | 4 / 4 / 3% | 11 / 10 / 45% | 47 / 28 / 99% |
| Standard, Ice Shards width holding the set | 6% | 60% | 99% |
| **Grim, the mixed line holding the set** | **32%** | **71%** | **99%** |

It can — sharply, and only as a set. And it showed what the plan had not
asked: the bonus was a relic for *everything*.

**2. The limit, then the split.** With Payload gated, what still lifts the Grim
guard is pierce alone — so width is bought with Payload and pierce stays low:

| Payload / Penetrators (gated) | 5 / 3 | 6 / 4 | 8 / 2 | 8 / 3 | 10 / 2 | **11 / 2** | 12 / 2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard, plain Bolts holding the set | 13% | 58% | 28% | 63% | 69% | **83%** | 91% |
| Grim, the mixed line holding the set | 29% | 37% | 24% | 32% | 25% | **24%** | 24% |

**3. The doors a big gated bonus could open**, in the table before the number
was chosen: finished Gatling Bolts with Payload alone (21% Standard, 3% Grim —
a lift, not a win); plain Mortars (0–1%, with the limit at "under 10"); Ice
Shards width holding the set (24%).

*(The search tables above were read before the instrument was corrected; the
answer table and the targets were read after. Rows that hold the full set from
wave 1 are the same on both — the set leaves six slots for offers either way.)*

**4. The no-patch diff failed twice, and was right to both times.**

- *10 of 15 rows differed.* Between the fit and the confirmation the limit had
  changed from "the folded hit is 9 or less" to "the tower's own hit is under
  10" (the Bastion case above). For a bare Bolt they are one rule — every row
  holding the full set was identical — but the lab takes option 0 of every
  relic offer, and a Bolt that had been dealt a flat `mods.damage` common fell
  out of the old rule and stays in the new one.
- *Re-fitted under the shipped code, 3 rows still differed by one seed each.*
  Penetrators' EPIC tier had been edited (5 → 4) while the first read was in
  flight, so the two sides came from different bundles. Which should not have
  mattered — and that is the finding below.

Read a third time, both sides and the 120-seed table from ONE bundle: **15
rows, 0 differ.**

## The lab's base world was richer than the app's

A base-world run cannot reach an epic tier: the band the tree bought caps what
an offer may deal, and the base world has bought none (D29). In the app. In the
lab, `runLab` passed the tree's `relicSlots` to the Sim and not its
`rarityMax`, so every base-world row of every table rolled rares (30 in 100)
and epics (10 and rising) from its offers. Same family as session 38's "the
lab's map for seed N was not the app's map".

What it moved, on 120 seeds with the same pool either side: the mixed line on
Standard 89% → **86%**, the base world on Grim 9% → **3%**, plain Bolt spam
6% → 2%, plain Ice Shards spam 8% → 4%. In the gate the Standard thermometer
fell about a wave, and is the one band that had to move; every other band
holds. D37's and D38's fits were read on the richer world; their
targets are still met on the corrected one, and the bands now say the
corrected numbers. Fixed in `lab.ts`, with `LabReport.relicsHeld` and a test
seen red first (a base-world run holding `iron_sights` and `bulk_order` at
rarity 2).

## What shipped

- `RelicEffects.damageAdd`, `damageAddBelow`, `armorPierce`; the fold; two
  lines in the sim; tests for each decision above, the Bastion case among them.
- The authoring surface in the same PR: `relics.schema.json` and the generated
  types, `patch.relics` in the fit harness, the codex and catalogue (generated),
  two placeholder icons.
- Two bands: *plain Bolts holding the set win Standard* (70–95% — a set that
  always wins is no longer a find) and *the set does not carry the base world
  through Grim* (≤ 30%).

## Not done, said plainly

- **Nobody has found this set in a run.** 83% is the six held from wave 1 — a
  ceiling. A base-world run offers three commons every two waves, out of a
  pool of thirty-eight; how often a player who *wants* the set assembles
  enough of it, and by which wave, is not measured. The gate's own words — "he finds a relic, goes
  wide with plain Bolts on purpose, and it works" — are a hands test.
- **Energy width enjoys Payload too** (an Ice Shard is a 4-damage hit): 23%
  holding the set, and plain Ice Shards spam reads 4% on Standard now that the
  pool can deal it one (the target is ≤ 10%). By D37's own logic a width build
  that relics carry is welcome in either type; it is recorded because it is
  the energy door again.
- **Standard's mixed line reads 86%** — the floor of the 85–95% it is held to,
  where D37 fitted it to the top on purpose (a person buys later and places
  worse than the lab). D38's twin took four points and the corrected
  instrument three. Energy-door's note deferred this to #366, and that reason
  was weak: #366 moves the tree's towers, not the base line. **It gets its own
  PR next — a curve fitted behind everything this one and the last changed.**
- **The Gatling fork wins Standard 2% with nothing held** — a dead fork under
  the new prices. That is #366's subject.
- Names (*Payload*, *Penetrators*) and the two icons are by default, his to
  overrule.
