`node tools/sweep.mjs` · Standard curve (hpLinear 0.18, geo 1.06, 6 + 4/wave) ·
five copies of one variant, auto-placed · no relics · wave 1 called at tick 0 ·
horizon 40. Death wave per seed; `>40` = survived the horizon.

Two questions this table answers (the design-round-1 gate asked for the
first; the D24 board change forced the second):

1. **Does a path dominate every seed?** The tower rework made each fork two
   roles. Within one tower, a path that is best-or-equal on every seed is a
   fork that is not a decision.
2. **What did the smaller board do?** 7×5 tiles (a 1920×1080 screen) against
   the old 12×7, same seeds, same knobs.

## Readings, not decisions

- **The smaller board is not harder across the board.** Bolt builds live
  *longer* on 7×5 (shorter lanes → the D18 offset lowers enemy health; five
  towers cover a larger share of the road). Mortar and frost die one to two
  waves earlier. The threat knobs do not need moving for the board change.
- **Three forks fail the gate as measured**: Hailstorm vs Railbore, Cluster
  vs Concussive, Absolute Zero vs Shatterfield. The first two are numbers
  (the multi-shot variants are under-tuned); the third is a measurement
  artefact (control cannot be scored solo) and needs a mixed-build sweep.
- The lab's analytic model still knows nothing of volleys, pierce, min range
  or the wave clock; only the headless runner is trusted here.

Proposed first retune, for Daniil's yes: Hailstorm and Cluster to 60% per
shot; Cluster's scatter 1.0 → 0.7 cells. Re-sweep after. The frost question
needs the sweep to place a bolt line and vary the frost alongside it.
