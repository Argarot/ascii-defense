line wins 99% — and session 40 found that on Grim six epics win for whatever
stands under them. So the rung is the same lines **holding nothing** (the lab
still takes option 0 of every offer, dealt inside the tree's rarity band):

## Targets, stated before any number is moved

- **A1 — a tree tower is never a downgrade.** Holding nothing, each of the
  Tesla, Missile and Laser lines wins **Standard at least 85%** (the base line
  under the tree: 94%) and **Grim at least as often as the base line under
  the tree** (26% today; whatever it reads after the change).
- **A2 — and none of them is the new answer to everything.** Holding nothing
  on Grim no single-tower tree line wins more than **70%**, the three sit
  within **30 points** of each other, and the mixed tree line wins at least as
  often as the best of them (mixing stays the better play — E3's spirit).
- **A3 — the closed doors stay shut.** Tesla width and Laser width still lose
  Standard (≤ 12%, the E1 band's ceiling); the base world still loses Grim
  (S4, ≤ 25% — it owns no tree tower, so only a shared rule could move it);
  mono-energy still loses to mixed holding the same relics (E3).
- **A4 — Payload's door.** Payload lifts a hit under 10; a plain Tesla hits
  for 9. Read `standard:teslaSpam` holding Payload before and after: it must
  not become a second synergy by accident.
- **What must not move:** every band of the gate that names no tree tower,
  and the golden replay hash.

**Method (CONTRIBUTING §5):** bound each lever at a value far too large before
tuning it — one kind of change a round — and read the doors in the same table
as the target.

round that said "stop and look at the rung".*

## The rung was unfair: the lines had no slow in them

CONTRIBUTING §5: *before believing a line is weak, check the plans are played
the same way.* They were not. The base line's tail is **Frost**, Railbore,
**Mortar**, Railbore; session 39's `tree*Deep` lines — the ones issue #366
quotes — replace **both** the Frost and the Mortar with one tower type. They
differ from the base line by a missing slow as much as by the tower.

The fair rung is the **slot**: the base line with the Mortar's place — the
line's area damage — given to the tree's tower, and nothing else changed
(`bareTeslaSlot`, `bareMissileSlot`, `bareLaserSlot`).

| in the Mortar's slot, holding nothing | Standard (80) | Grim (80) | **Grim (200)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mortar** — the base line under the tree | 94% | 26% | **21%** |
| Tesla (Long Arc, Forked, Overload) | 95% | 23% | **19%** |
| Missile Rack (Warhead, Fragmentation, Bunker Buster) | 88% | 18% | **14%** |
| Laser Lance (Capacitor, Fast Cycle, Cutter) | 96% | 42% | **42%** |

(A rate near 20% read on 200 seeds carries about ±3 points; on 80, about ±5.)

**Most of #366 was the instrument.** "Tesla line 16%, Missile line 9%" was
two lines with no Frost in them. Played in a slot, the Tesla is the Mortar's
equal (19% against 21%, inside the noise), the Laser is the best thing a
player can put there (+21 points), and every one of them wins Standard. A1's
Standard half is **met as shipped**; its Grim half is met by the Tesla and the
Laser. **What is left is the Missile Rack: 7 points under the Mortar on Grim.**

## The Missile Rack: five levers bounded, none reaches the Mortar

Grim, the Missile in the slot, 200 seeds unless said; the Mortar reads 21%
(26% on 80):

| the lever, at a value far too large | wins Grim | wins Standard |
|---|---|---|
| as shipped | 14% | 90% |
| price ÷2 (125) — *80 seeds* | 21% | 94% |
| damage ×3 (90 a missile) — *80 seeds* | 23% | 94% |
| fire rate ×2 (a missile every 1.5 s) | 17% | 95% |
| blast radius ×2.5 | 15% | 92% |
| flight speed ×3 | 13% | 91% |
| *its other forks, as shipped:* Warhead, Salvo, Bunker Buster | 13% | 91% |
| *…* Seeker, Salvo, Barrage | 9% | 84% |

Three times the damage, on the 80-seed corpus where the Mortar reads 26%,
reads 23%. **No number the Missile Rack owns puts it level with a Mortar in
that slot on Grim.** The Mortar the line buys is *Concussive* — it slows — and
the Frost beside it slows; what holds Grim's waves 15–25 for the base world is
control stacked on control, and the Missile Rack is damage however it is
shaped. That is not a defect in a number. It is a question about what the
tower is **for**, and it is put to Daniil as a call with a default rather than
answered at night by inventing a mechanic for it (issue #392).

## What ships, and what does not

**No content number moves.** Nothing tuned met a stated target, and a number
that misses its target is not shipped to look busy.

- **The rung:** `bare*Slot`, `bareBaseDeep`, `bareRails`, `bare*Deep`,
  `teslaSpamPayload` and the Missile's two other forks join the lab's one
  table, with the story of why the slot is the fair reading. The ladder's tree
  rungs play the slot lines; the `tree*Deep` lines stay in the table, marked
  for what they are.
- **The gate** gets the three bands that make "a tree tower is never a
  downgrade" an alarm instead of a sentence: the weakest slot's floor on
  Standard (Missile), the base line under the tree on Grim, and the strongest
  slot's ceiling on Grim (Laser — A2's guard against a new answer to
  everything).
- **Targets, as read:** A1 Standard **met** (95 / 88 / 96% against 85%). A1
  Grim **met** by Tesla (≈) and Laser, **missed** by the Missile Rack (−7) and
  recorded as missed. A2 **met** (no slot over 70%; 14–42%, a 28-point
  spread; the mixed tree line is not read in a slot — see "Not done"). A3
  **met** and unmoved (Tesla width 0%, Laser width 6%, base world 3%, E3 84%).
  A4 **read**: Tesla width holding Payload wins Standard **10%** — a door ajar,
  not open; it is under E1's 12% ceiling and is now a row anyone can re-read.
